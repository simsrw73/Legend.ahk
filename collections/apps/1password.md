# 1Password

1Password 8. Quick Access works from any app.

## Quick Access
- `Ctrl+Shift+Space` Open or close Quick Access
- `Ctrl+Shift+C` Copy password
- `Ctrl+Alt+C` Copy one-time password
- `Alt+Enter` Open the website and fill the login
- `Ctrl+Shift+O` Open the item in the app

## Items
- `Ctrl+Shift+C` Copy password
- `Ctrl+Alt+C` Copy one-time password
- `Ctrl+Shift+F` Open the website and fill the login
- `Ctrl+E` Edit item
- `Ctrl+R` Reveal or conceal secure fields
- `Delete` Move to Archive

## Navigate
- `Ctrl+Alt+F` Find in the current list
- `Ctrl+1` All accounts
- `Ctrl+2–9` Switch account or collection
- `Ctrl+Shift+D` Collapse or expand the sidebar
- `Ctrl+Shift+/` Show all shortcuts
